Paste a suspicious email. Understand the warning signs.

This tool reads the text of an email and highlights the tricks attackers use — urgency, fake log-in requests, look-alike links, dangerous attachments and failed mail authentication. You get an explainable risk score, the reason behind every point, and clear next steps.

Your privacy is protected by design

All analysis happens inside your web browser. The text you paste is never uploaded, saved, logged or shared — there is no server, no database, no analytics and no third-party service involved. Close the tab and it is gone. You can even disconnect from the internet and the tool will still work.

What this tool is — and is not

This is an educational triage aid. It identifies warning signs; it cannot prove an email is malicious or safe. A quiet email can still be dangerous. Use it to learn and to slow down — never as your only decision-maker.

1 Paste the suspicious email

Include the subject line and, if you have them, the full raw headers (From, Reply-To, Received-SPF, Authentication-Results). Headers let the tool check for sender mismatches and SPF/DKIM/DMARC failures. Never open links or attachments to test them — this tool only reads the text you paste.
